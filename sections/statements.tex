\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work concerns risk-controlled early stopping of medical-image acquisition. The prostate \textsc{mri} data were analyzed under an institutional IRB-approved research protocol as a retrospective study of pre-existing, de-identified clinical examinations from patients evaluated for suspected prostate cancer; no patients were enrolled and no imaging was performed for research. Splits were made at the patient level so that no patient appears in more than one of the training, validation, calibration, and test sets. \gls{method} is meant to support clinical judgment, not replace it, and any deployment would require prospective validation; in particular, faster screening can enlarge the screened population and so raise the absolute number of false positives. By design, samples that are not stopped early proceed to the existing full-acquisition protocol. The stopped-sample certificate does not establish noninferiority of the complete hybrid workflow to standard practice; this would require separate end-to-end and prospective clinical validation.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
The calibration procedure is specified in \cref{sec:rcs}, with pseudocode in \cref{eq:alg_ltt} and the proof of \cref{thm:conditional_risk} in \cref{appsec:proofs}. The three policy families (confidence threshold, \textsc{jafa} Q-margin, ambiguity bands) are defined in \cref{sec:rcs} and \cref{app:ambiguity_bands}. Dataset construction, acquisition ordering, and split statistics are given in \cref{sec:experiments_1} and the appendix. \textsc{cifar}-10 and \textsc{fastMRI} are public; code for the calibration procedure and experiments will be released as anonymized supplementary material.
